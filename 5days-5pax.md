5 DAYS LUXURY FLY-IN SAFARI
Arusha – Serengeti – Ngorongoro Crater
Day 1: Arrival in Arusha – Welcome to Tanzania
Upon arrival at Kilimanjaro International Airport or Arusha Airport, you will be warmly
welcomed by our representative and transferred to your luxury hotel in Arusha.
Arusha is the gateway to Tanzania’s northern safari circuit, nestled at the foothills of Mount
Meru.The rest of the day is at leisure to relax after your international flight. Enjoy fine dining
and tranquil surroundings at your hotel.
 Arusha Coffee Lodge
Day 2: Fly to Serengeti National Park
After breakfast, transfer to Arusha Airstrip for your scheduled scenic bush flight to Serengeti
National Park.
The flight offers spectacular aerial views of the Great Rift Valley and vast savannah plains.
Upon arrival at the Serengeti airstrip, you will be met by your private safari guide and begin
your first game drive en route to your luxury lodge or tented camp.
The Serengeti is world-renowned for:The Great Migration (seasonal),Large prides of
lions,Cheetahs and leopards and Vast herds of elephants, zebras, and wildebeest
Afternoon game drive followed by sunset relaxation at camp.
Accommodation (Full Board): Four Seasons Safari Lodge Serengeti
Day 3: Full Day in Serengeti
Enjoy a full day exploring the endless plains of the Serengeti with morning and afternoon
game drives.Your guide will tailor the safari according to wildlife movements to maximize
sightings.
Optional experience (at additional cost): Hot Air Balloon Safari over the Serengeti at sunrise
followed by champagne bush breakfast.Return to your lodge for dinner and overnight.
Accommodation (Full Board): Four seasons Safari lodge Serengeti
Day 4: Serengeti to Ngorongoro Conservation Area
After breakfast and a final morning game drive, depart for Ngorongoro Conservation Area.
Enjoy a scenic drive through the highlands with breathtaking landscapes and Maasai villages
along the way. Arrive at your luxury lodge located on or near the crater rim.
Accommodation (Full Board): Lions Paw
Day 5: Ngorongoro Crater Safari & Departure
Early breakfast followed by descent into the spectacular Ngorongoro Crater, a UNESCO
World Heritage Site and one of Africa’s most extraordinary wildlife havens.
The crater hosts, Black rhino (rare sightings), Large lion populations, Buffalo, elephants,
hippos, Abundant birdlife
Enjoy a picnic lunch on the crater floor. In the afternoon, drive back to Arusha for your
onward flight.
End of Safari.
