# City Tour Vehicle

## 🏙️ Service Introduction

Comfortable city tour vehicles equipped with Chinese or English-speaking guides for in-depth exploration of Tanzania's urban culture and history.

## 🚐 Vehicle Configuration

### Standard Vehicle
*   **Business MPV**: Toyota Alphard / Mercedes V-Class
*   **Seating**: 6-7 seats (including driver and guide)
*   **Features**: Air conditioning, leather seats, audio system, WiFi hotspot

### Pricing Reference
*   **Half-day Tour (4 hours)**: $120
*   **Full-day Tour (8 hours)**: $200
*   **Multi-day Charter**: $180/day (includes driver, excludes guide)
*   **Chinese-speaking Guide Fee**: $80/day

## 🗺️ Popular City Tour Routes

### Arusha City Tour (Half-day)
*   Clock Tower (East African Center Point Monument)
*   Cultural Heritage Centre
*   Maasai Market (local market)
*   Coffee Plantation Visit
*   **Price**: $150 (1-6 people, includes guide)

### Stone Town In-depth Tour (Full-day)
*   Stone Town Old Town Walking Tour
*   Freddie Mercury House
*   Old Fort & Palace Museum
*   Spice Market (Darajani Market)
*   Sunset Sailing (Dhow)
*   **Price**: $180 (1-6 people, includes guide + dhow)

### Dar es Salaam Historical & Cultural Tour
*   National Museum
*   Village Museum
*   Kivukoni Fish Market
*   Coco Beach Promenade
*   **Price**: $160 (1-6 people, includes guide)

## 🎯 Value-Added Services

*   **Restaurant Reservation**: Assistance booking local specialty restaurants (free)
*   **Shopping Companion**: Visit Tanzanite shops, wood carving markets (free)
*   **Photography Service**: Professional photographer accompanies ( $100/day)
*   **Child Seats**: Safety seats provided (free, advance notice required)

## 🕒 Service Hours

*   Standard Hours: 08:00 - 18:00
*   Overtime Fee: $25/hour
*   Night Service (after 18:00): 30% surcharge

✓ Driver fees
✓ Vehicle fuel
✓ City parking fees
✓ Vehicle insurance
✓ Mineral water

## ❌ Not Included

✗ Entrance fees
✗ Meal expenses
✗ Guide tips (recommended $10-20/day)
