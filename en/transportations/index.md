# [ Module: Transportation ]

We provide professional transportation services throughout Tanzania, from Safari game drives to city tours, from airport transfers to self-drive adventures.

## [ Safari Vehicles ]

- [**Safari Game Drive 4x4**](safari-4x4)
  *   > Professionally modified, open-top design, 7-seat configuration, equipped with refrigerator and charging devices.

- [**Self-Drive Camping 4x4**](self-drive-camping)
  *   > Complete camping equipment, roof-top tent, suitable for experienced adventurers.

## [ City Transportation ]

- [**Airport Transfer Service**](airport-transfer)
  *   > Covering major airports including JRO, DAR, ZNZ, multiple vehicle types available.

- [**City Tour Vehicle**](city-tour)
  *   > Business MPV with professional guide, in-depth exploration of city culture.

## 💡 Booking Tips

*   **Advance Booking**: During peak season (June-October), we recommend booking vehicles 1-2 months in advance
*   **Flexible Combinations**: Different transportation methods can be combined according to your itinerary
*   **Professional Drivers**: All drivers hold professional licenses and are fluent in English (some speak Chinese)
