# Self-Drive Camping 4x4

## 🏕️ Vehicle Overview

Suitable for adventurers with extensive off-road and camping experience. We provide **Toyota Land Cruiser** or **Land Rover Defender** equipped with complete camping gear.

### Standard Equipment

#### Vehicle Equipment
*   **Roof-Top Tent**: 2-3 person capacity, includes mattress and sleeping bags
*   **Auxiliary Fuel Tank**: Total fuel capacity 200L+, range over 1000 km
*   **Winch & Recovery Gear**: High-lift jack, tow rope, sand tracks
*   **GPS Navigation System**: Pre-loaded with park routes and campsite coordinates
*   **Vehicle Refrigerator/Freezer**: 60L capacity

#### Camping Equipment
*   Folding table and chairs (4-person set)
*   Stove and gas canisters
*   Cookware set (pots, bowls, utensils, etc.)
*   10L water container x 2
*   Emergency food and drinking water
*   Camp lighting (LED lights + solar charging panel)

### Pricing Reference

*   **Vehicle Rental**: $180-250 / day
*   **Camping Equipment Package**: $50 / day
*   **GPS & Satellite Phone Rental**: $15 / day
*   **Security Deposit**: $2000 (credit card pre-authorization)

## ⚠️ Rental Requirements

*   International driving permit + original driver's license
*   Minimum 3 years driving experience
*   Age 25 or above
*   Sign liability waiver
*   Full insurance strongly recommended (includes roadside assistance)

## 📋 Recommended Routes

### Northern Circuit Self-Drive (7-10 days)
Arusha → Tarangire → Lake Manyara → Ngorongoro → Serengeti → Return to Arusha

### Southern Adventure Route (12-14 days)
Dar es Salaam → Mikumi → Ruaha → Nyerere → Return to Dar es Salaam

> ⚠️ **Important Notes**:
> - Western Circuit (Katavi, Mahale) not recommended for self-drive (extremely poor roads and no fuel stations)
> - Rainy season (March-May) not recommended for self-drive Safari
> - Campsites must be booked in advance (park campsites do not accept walk-ins)
