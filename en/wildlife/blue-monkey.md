## 🐒 Blue Monkey - The Shy Hermit of the Forest

### Overview

Despite being called "Blue Monkey," the fur color is actually closer to olive-gray. They are very shy, usually active in the canopy, difficult to spot. Morning calls can travel far, used to declare territory. Blue monkeys are important members of forest ecosystems, maintaining forest health by dispersing seeds and pollen.

### Physical Characteristics

#### Body Features
- **Size**: Males 4-8kg, females 3-6kg
- **Length**: 50-65cm (excluding tail)
- **Tail**: About 60-80cm long
- **Fur**: Blue-gray or olive-gray, lighter belly

#### Unique Features
- **Face**: Blue-gray face with white fur
- **Vocal Sac**: Males have vocal sacs, can make loud calls
- **Eyes**: Large round orange eyes
- **Limbs**: Long slender limbs, suitable for arboreal activity

### Behavior and Habits

#### Feeding Behavior
- **Omnivorous**: Eat fruits, leaves, insects, small animals
- **Arboreal**: Almost entirely arboreal
- **Diurnal**: Active during day, rest at night
- **Feeding Time**: Mainly feed at dawn and dusk

#### Social Structure
- **Group Living**: Form groups of 10-40 individuals
- **One Male Multiple Females**: Usually consist of one male and multiple females
- **Hierarchy**: Strict hierarchy within groups
- **Territorial Awareness**: Mark territory through vocalizations

#### Communication
- **Vocalizations**: Morning calls can travel far
- **Facial Expressions**: Use facial expressions for communication
- **Body Language**: Use gestures and postures for communication

### Safari Observation Tips

#### Best Viewing Locations
- **Arusha National Park**: Forest areas
- **Lake Manyara**: Forest areas
- **Ruaha National Park**: Forest areas

#### Observation Techniques
- **Morning Observation**: Morning is when blue monkeys are most active
- **Listen for Sounds**: Locate blue monkeys through vocalizations
- **Stay Quiet**: Blue monkeys are very shy, easily startled
- **Look for Canopy**: Blue monkeys usually active in canopy
