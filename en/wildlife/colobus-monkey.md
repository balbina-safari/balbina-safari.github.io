## 🐒 Black and White Colobus - The Black and White Knight of the Forest

### Overview

The Black and White Colobus is a very photogenic beautiful monkey, famous for its unique black and white contrasting colors and long white "cape." The long white tail fur looks like a cloak, creating a dramatic visual impact when jumping through trees. They mainly eat leaves, with a specialized multi-chambered stomach that can digest cellulose. The name "Colobus" comes from Greek, meaning "mutilated," because they lack thumbs, an evolutionary adaptation for arboreal life.

### Physical Characteristics

#### Body Features
- **Size**: Males 9-14kg, females 7-10kg
- **Length**: 50-70cm (excluding tail)
- **Tail**: About 70-90cm long, with white fur at the end
- **Fur**: Black body, white "cape" and tail fur

#### Unique Features
- **Black and White Contrast**: Black body with white long fur on back and tail, creating strong black and white contrast
- **No Thumbs**: Lack thumbs, only four fingers, evolutionary adaptation for arboreal life
- **Long Tail Fur**: Long white fur at the end of tail, looks like a cloak
- **Face**: Black face surrounded by white fur

### Behavior and Habits

#### Feeding Behavior
- **Mainly Leaves**: Mainly eat leaves, 60-70% of diet
- **Specialized Stomach**: Multi-chambered stomach can digest cellulose
- **Diurnal**: Active during day, rest at night
- **Arboreal**: Almost entirely arboreal, rarely come to ground

#### Social Structure
- **Group Living**: Form small groups of 3-15 individuals
- **One Male Multiple Females**: Usually consist of one male and multiple females
- **Matrilineal Society**: Females are the stable core of the group
- **Territorial Awareness**: Mark territory through vocalizations

#### Movement
- **Jumping**: Jump between trees, can jump over 10 meters
- **Balance**: Use long tail for balance
- **Silent**: Move almost silently, avoid detection by predators

### Safari Observation Tips

#### Best Viewing Locations
- **Arusha National Park**: Forest areas
- **Lake Manyara**: Forest areas
- **Ruaha National Park**: Forest areas

#### Observation Techniques
- **Look for Canopy**: Colobus monkeys usually active in canopy
- **Notice White Tail**: Long white tail is easy to spot
- **Stay Quiet**: Colobus monkeys are shy, easily startled
- **Be Patient**: Colobus monkeys may rest in trees, need patience to wait
