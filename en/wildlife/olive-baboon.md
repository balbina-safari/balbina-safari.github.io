## 🐒 Olive Baboon - The Wise Leader of the Savanna

### Overview

The Olive Baboon is a very common and adaptable primate, with groups reaching up to 150 individuals. They have a strict hierarchy, with alpha males having mating priority. Olive baboons are highly intelligent, capable of learning simple tool use. They are one of the most successful primates on African savannas, capable of surviving in various environments.

### Physical Characteristics

#### Body Features
- **Size**: Males 25-30kg, females 12-16kg
- **Length**: 50-110cm (excluding tail)
- **Tail**: About 40-60cm long
- **Fur**: Green-brown or olive, males have dense mane

#### Unique Features
- **Face**: Canine-like elongated snout, red or purple face
- **Buttocks**: Red buttocks for attracting mates
- **Canine Teeth**: Males have huge canine teeth for fighting
- **Mane**: Males have dense mane, displaying masculine characteristics

### Behavior and Habits

#### Social Structure
- **Large Groups**: Groups can reach 150 individuals
- **Hierarchy**: Strict hierarchy, alpha males have mating priority
- **Male Alliances**: Males form alliances to defend territory together
- **Matrilineal Society**: Females usually stay in birth groups

#### Feeding Behavior
- **Omnivorous**: Eat fruits, leaves, insects, small animals, crops
- **Terrestrial Foraging**: Often forage on ground
- **Opportunistic**: Will steal food and attack livestock

#### Intelligence
- **Tool Use**: Capable of learning simple tool use
- **Social Learning**: Young monkeys learn survival skills through observation
- **Problem Solving**: Capable of solving complex problems

### Safari Observation Tips

#### Best Viewing Locations
- **Common in All Parks**: Olive baboons are common in all parks
- **Near Camps**: Often active near camps

#### Observation Techniques
- **Observe Groups**: Olive baboons usually appear in large groups
- **Identify Alpha**: Pay attention to alpha male behavior
- **Keep Distance**: Olive baboons can be aggressive, maintain safe distance
- **Secure Belongings**: Carefully secure food and belongings to avoid theft
