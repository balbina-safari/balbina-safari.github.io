## 🐱 Serval - The Elegant Hunter of Wetlands

### Overview

The Serval has the longest legs proportionally and largest ears among cats, with extremely sensitive hearing that can detect rodents underground. They mainly inhabit wetlands and tall grass areas, with golden base color and black spots, very beautiful. 80% of serval hunting occurs at night, making them important predators in wetland ecosystems.

### Physical Characteristics

#### Body Features
- **Size**: Males 9-18kg, females 9-15kg
- **Length**: 60-100cm (excluding tail)
- **Tail**: About 30-40cm long
- **Fur**: Golden base color with black spots, white belly

#### Unique Features
- **Extremely Long Legs**: Longest legs proportionally among cats, suitable for activity in tall grass
- **Large Ears**: Largest ears proportionally among cats, extremely sensitive hearing
- **Spots**: Golden base color with black spots, very beautiful
- **Face**: Elongated face with large eyes

### Behavior and Habits

#### Hunting Behavior
- **Jump Hunting**: Can jump 3 meters high to catch birds
- **Nocturnal**: 80% of hunting occurs at night
- **Ambush**: Stalk prey then suddenly attack
- **Success Rate**: Servals have high hunting success rate, about 50%

#### Feeding Behavior
- **Main Diet**: Mainly eat rodents, birds, reptiles
- **Opportunistic**: Also eat insects, small mammals
- **Food Intake**: Adult servals need 1-2kg of food daily

#### Social Structure
- **Solitary**: Servals are solitary animals, except during mating season
- **Territorial Awareness**: Males are territorial, mark through scent
- **Mating**: Females can come into estrus year-round, gestation about 70 days

### Safari Observation Tips

#### Best Viewing Locations
- **Serengeti Wetland Areas**: Best location for serval observation
- **Tall Grass Areas**: Servals like to be active in tall grass areas

#### Observation Techniques
- **Night Observation**: Night is the best time to observe servals
- **Look for Wetlands**: Servals like to be active in wetlands and tall grass areas
- **Stay Quiet**: Servals are very shy, easily startled
- **Be Patient**: Servals may stay motionless for hours
